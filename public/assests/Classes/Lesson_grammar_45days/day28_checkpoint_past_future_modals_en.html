📖 1. Detailed Rules & Spelling Patterns

Past be: was/were

Recall agreement of past be forms.

is → was
The room was quiet yesterday.
💡 Room is singular and yesterday is past.
are → were
Our friends were excited last night.
💡 Friends is plural, so use were.

Regular past: spelling patterns

Check -ed, -d, consonant+y, and consonant doubling.

study → studied
I studied after dinner.
💡 Study ends consonant d + y, so change y to ied.
stop → stopped
The rain stopped at noon.
💡 Stop doubles p after its short vowel.

Irregular verbs and did

Use V2 in affirmative, V1 after did or didn’t.

take → took
He took my photo yesterday.
💡 Took is irregular V2.
went → did not go
They did not go to school on Sunday.
💡 Did marks past; go remains V1.

Will vs going to

Distinguish instant decisions from prior plans or clear evidence.

instant decision → will help
I will help you carry that box.
💡 The offer is made now, so will fits.
prior plan → going to visit
We are going to visit the museum on Saturday.
💡 The trip is a plan already made.

Can/could and bare infinitive

Keep the main verb in its base form after modal verbs.

can + ride → can ride
She can ride a bicycle.
💡 Can is followed by ride, not rides.
could + read → could read
I could read at age five.
💡 Could conveys general ability in the past.

Negatives and questions review

Use the appropriate auxiliary: did, will, or can.

went → Did ... go?
Did she go to the zoo?
💡 Did means the main verb returns to go.
will → won’t
They won’t come tonight.
💡 Won’t negates will and is followed by base come.

⚠️ 3. Common Mistakes: Wrong vs Right

MISTAKE 1
❌ We was outside.
✅ We were outside.
💡 Room is singular and yesterday is past.
MISTAKE 2
❌ She studyd yesterday.
✅ She studied yesterday.
💡 Study ends consonant d + y, so change y to ied.
MISTAKE 3
❌ He didn’t took it.
✅ He didn’t take it.
💡 Took is irregular V2.
MISTAKE 4
❌ I will to carry it.
✅ I will carry it.
💡 The offer is made now, so will fits.
