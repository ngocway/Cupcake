📖 1. Detailed Rules & Spelling Patterns

Short superlatives: the + -est

Use the with a short adjective ending in -est.

tall → the tallest
Ben is the tallest boy in our class.
💡 Tall adds -est to identify the extreme within a group.
small → the smallest
This is the smallest kitten.
💡 Small takes -est with the when selecting the extreme.

Final -e and consonant + y

Add -st after e; change consonant+y to -iest.

large → the largest
That is the largest room.
💡 Large already ends in e; add st only.
happy → the happiest
She is the happiest child here.
💡 Happy has consonant p before y, so y becomes iest.

CVC doubles before -est

Double a final consonant after a stressed short vowel.

big → the biggest
That is the biggest pumpkin.
💡 Big doubles g before -est.
hot → the hottest
August is the hottest month here.
💡 Hot doubles t before -est.

Long superlatives: the most

Use the most + long adjective.

interesting → the most interesting
This is the most interesting story.
💡 Interesting is too long for the regular -est pattern.
beautiful → the most beautiful
That is the most beautiful picture.
💡 Beautiful takes the most, not beautifulest.

Irregular superlatives

Good becomes best; bad becomes worst.

good → the best
This is the best answer.
💡 Good changes irregularly to best.
bad → the worst
That was the worst storm this year.
💡 Bad has irregular superlative worst.

As...as and not as...as

Use the base adjective between two as words; add not for inequality.

fast → as fast as
The red bike is as fast as the blue bike.
💡 Equality takes the base fast, not faster.
tall → not as tall as
I am not as tall as my sister.
💡 Not as...as shows a lower degree; tall remains unchanged.

⚠️ 3. Common Mistakes: Wrong vs Right

MISTAKE 1
❌ She is the most tall in class.
✅ She is the tallest in class.
💡 Tall adds -est to identify the extreme within a group.
MISTAKE 2
❌ That is the largeest room.
✅ That is the largest room.
💡 Large already ends in e; add st only.
MISTAKE 3
❌ This is the bigest dog.
✅ This is the biggest dog.
💡 Big doubles g before -est.
MISTAKE 4
❌ It is the beautifulest place.
✅ It is the most beautiful place.
💡 Interesting is too long for the regular -est pattern.
