📖 1. Detailed Rules & Spelling Patterns

Tenses: select by meaning

Distinguish ongoing, finished, routine and present-result situations.

habit → reads
He reads every night.
💡 Every night signals a habit requiring third-person -s.
current result → have lost
I have lost my notebook.
💡 The notebook is still missing, so the result matters now.

Past forms and sequencing

Distinguish completed events and background events.

go → went
We went hiking yesterday.
💡 Yesterday fixes the action in a completed past time.
was walking + saw
I was walking when I saw a fox.
💡 Walking was ongoing; seeing was a shorter event.

Future and modals

Use will for instant decisions and must for strong rules.

offer → will carry
I will carry your bag.
💡 The decision is made during the conversation.
rule → must wear
You must wear a helmet.
💡 Must marks an important obligation; wear is base form.

Comparisons and degree

Choose the comparative or superlative pattern by the number compared.

good → better
This book is better than mine.
💡 Good has irregular comparative better.
beautiful → most beautiful
This is the most beautiful flower here.
💡 Long beautiful uses the most for an extreme in a group.

Advanced structures: passive and relative

Recognize receivers of actions and defining information about nouns.

build → was built
The library was built in 2012.
💡 The library receives the action and 2012 indicates past time.
student + who
The student who won is my friend.
💡 Who defines which person is meant.

Conditionals and reported speech

Identify realistic future conditions, unreal hypotheticals and reported statements.

if + present → will
If it rains, we will stay home.
💡 A possible future condition uses present after if and will in the result.
will → would
She said she would come later.
💡 Will backshifts to would after past said in this reported context.

⚠️ 3. Common Mistakes: Wrong vs Right

MISTAKE 1
❌ He reads now. (action in progress intended)
✅ He is reading now.
💡 Every night signals a habit requiring third-person -s.
MISTAKE 2
❌ We goed there yesterday.
✅ We went there yesterday.
💡 Yesterday fixes the action in a completed past time.
MISTAKE 3
❌ He must wears a helmet.
✅ He must wear a helmet.
💡 The decision is made during the conversation.
MISTAKE 4
❌ She is the most tall in class.
✅ She is the tallest in class.
💡 Good has irregular comparative better.
