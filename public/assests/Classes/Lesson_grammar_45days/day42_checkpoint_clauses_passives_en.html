📖 1. Detailed Rules & Spelling Patterns

Present perfect with since/for

Select since for points and for for durations.

2019 → since 2019
I have lived here since 2019.
💡 2019 is a start point; the state still continues.
two months → for two months
She has studied here for two months.
💡 Two months is a duration; she takes has.

Present perfect or finished past

Look for a definite completed past time.

yesterday → saw
I saw him yesterday.
💡 Yesterday requires past simple, not present perfect.
ever → have ... seen
Have you ever seen a dolphin?
💡 Ever asks about life experience without a finished time.

Passive: be + V3

Choose the tense of be and the correct past participle.

make → is made
This cheese is made locally.
💡 Cheese is singular and present passive takes is + made.
build → was built
The school was built in 2005.
💡 In 2005 requires past passive was + built.

Zero and first conditional

Use present-present for truths; present-will for real future outcomes.

general → melts
Ice melts if it gets warm.
💡 This is a general scientific pattern.
possible future → will play
If it stops raining, we will play outside.
💡 A possible future event leads to a future result.

Second conditional and wish

Use past-form conditions with would, or wish + past for unreal present.

if + had → would buy
If I had money, I would buy a bike.
💡 The money situation is hypothetical, not a past fact.
wish + could
I wish I could fly.
💡 The speaker wishes for a present ability they lack.

Defining relative pronouns

Use who for people, which for things and whose for possession.

person → who
The student who won is here.
💡 Who identifies a person.
owner → whose
The boy whose bike broke is sad.
💡 Whose identifies the owner of the bicycle.

⚠️ 3. Common Mistakes: Wrong vs Right

MISTAKE 1
❌ I have lived here for 2019.
✅ I have lived here since 2019.
💡 2019 is a start point; the state still continues.
MISTAKE 2
❌ I have seen him yesterday.
✅ I saw him yesterday.
💡 Yesterday requires past simple, not present perfect.
MISTAKE 3
❌ The bridge is build every year.
✅ The bridge is built every year.
💡 Cheese is singular and present passive takes is + made.
MISTAKE 4
❌ If it will stop raining, we will go out.
✅ If it stops raining, we will go out.
💡 This is a general scientific pattern.
