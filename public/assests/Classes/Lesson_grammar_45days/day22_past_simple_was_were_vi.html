🗺️ SƠ ĐỒ TƯ DUY: Past Simple: Was / Were (Be in Past)
Was với I/he/she/it
is → was
She was at the library yesterday.
💡 She là số ít; yesterday xác định trạng thái ở quá khứ.
am → was
I was tired after the match.
💡 I dùng was chứ không dùng were trong câu kể quá khứ.
Phủ định: was not / were not
was → was not
The classroom was not noisy this morning.
💡 Not đứng sau was; không thêm did với was.
were → were not
They were not late for practice.
💡 Were not phủ định trạng thái quá khứ số nhiều.
Trả lời ngắn
Was...? → Yes, ... was
Yes, she was.
💡 Câu hỏi Was phải trả lời bằng was, không dùng did.
Were...? → No, ... were not
No, we were not.
💡 Câu trả lời giữ were để hòa hợp với we.

📖 1. Quy tắc chi tiết & Biến đổi dạng từ

Was với I/he/she/it

Dùng was với chủ ngữ số ít ở quá khứ.

is → was
She was at the library yesterday.
💡 She là số ít; yesterday xác định trạng thái ở quá khứ.
am → was
I was tired after the match.
💡 I dùng was chứ không dùng were trong câu kể quá khứ.

Were với you/we/they

Dùng were với you và chủ ngữ số nhiều.

are → were
We were at the zoo last Sunday.
💡 We là số nhiều và last Sunday đã kết thúc.
are → were
You were very helpful yesterday.
💡 You luôn dùng were dù chỉ một hay nhiều người.

Phủ định: was not / were not

Đặt not ngay sau động từ be quá khứ.

was → was not
The classroom was not noisy this morning.
💡 Not đứng sau was; không thêm did với was.
were → were not
They were not late for practice.
💡 Were not phủ định trạng thái quá khứ số nhiều.

Câu hỏi: Was/Were + chủ ngữ

Đảo was hoặc were lên trước chủ ngữ.

she was → Was she...?
Was she at home last night?
💡 Chính be quá khứ tạo câu hỏi nên không cần did.
they were → Were they...?
Were they ready for the show?
💡 They đi với were khi đảo lên đầu câu.

Trả lời ngắn

Lặp lại was hoặc were trong câu trả lời ngắn.

Was...? → Yes, ... was
Yes, she was.
💡 Câu hỏi Was phải trả lời bằng was, không dùng did.
Were...? → No, ... were not
No, we were not.
💡 Câu trả lời giữ were để hòa hợp với we.

🎯 2. Tình huống thực tế

Ở trường

She was at the library yesterday.
You were very helpful yesterday.

Ở nhà và với bạn bè

The classroom was not noisy this morning.
Were they ready for the show?

Kế hoạch và cuộc sống hằng ngày

Yes, she was.
I was tired after the match.

⚠️ 3. Lỗi thường gặp: Sai vs Đúng

LỖI 1
❌ She were tired.
✅ She was tired.
💡 She là số ít; yesterday xác định trạng thái ở quá khứ.
LỖI 2
❌ They was ready.
✅ They were ready.
💡 We là số nhiều và last Sunday đã kết thúc.
LỖI 3
❌ I did not was happy.
✅ I was not happy.
💡 Not đứng sau was; không thêm did với was.
LỖI 4
❌ Did you were late?
✅ Were you late?
💡 Chính be quá khứ tạo câu hỏi nên không cần did.
