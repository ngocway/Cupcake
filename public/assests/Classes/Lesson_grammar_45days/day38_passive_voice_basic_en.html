🗺️ GRAMMAR MAP: Passive Voice: Present & Past Simple
Present passive: am/is/are + V3
make → is made
Bread is made here every day.
💡 Bread receives the action, and singular bread takes is.
clean → are cleaned
The rooms are cleaned daily.
💡 Rooms is plural, so the passive uses are + cleaned.
Active to passive transformation
They wash the car → The car is washed
The car is washed every weekend.
💡 The receiver the car becomes the grammatical subject.
They painted the wall → The wall was painted
The wall was painted yesterday.
💡 A completed past action requires was + painted.
Passive negatives and questions
is used → is not used
This door is not used at night.
💡 Not follows is; used stays V3.
Was it built? → Was ... built?
Was the school built in 2010?
💡 Was moves before the subject; built remains V3.

📖 1. Detailed Rules & Spelling Patterns

Present passive: am/is/are + V3

Use present be and past participle when the receiver is central.

make → is made
Bread is made here every day.
💡 Bread receives the action, and singular bread takes is.
clean → are cleaned
The rooms are cleaned daily.
💡 Rooms is plural, so the passive uses are + cleaned.

Past passive: was/were + V3

Past be marks the tense, V3 marks passive voice.

build → was built
The bridge was built in 1990.
💡 The bridge is singular, and in 1990 is finished past.
invite → were invited
The students were invited yesterday.
💡 Students is plural, so use were and V3 invited.

Active to passive transformation

Move the object into subject position; adjust be and use V3.

They wash the car → The car is washed
The car is washed every weekend.
💡 The receiver the car becomes the grammatical subject.
They painted the wall → The wall was painted
The wall was painted yesterday.
💡 A completed past action requires was + painted.

By + agent when relevant

Include by + doer only when that information matters.

written + by → was written by
The story was written by my sister.
💡 By identifies the specific author.
made + by → was made by
The cake was made by Dad.
💡 The doer Dad is relevant to this statement.

Passive negatives and questions

Put not after be; invert be for questions.

is used → is not used
This door is not used at night.
💡 Not follows is; used stays V3.
Was it built? → Was ... built?
Was the school built in 2010?
💡 Was moves before the subject; built remains V3.

⚠️ 3. Common Mistakes: Wrong vs Right

MISTAKE 1
❌ The rooms is cleaned daily.
✅ The rooms are cleaned daily.
💡 Bread receives the action, and singular bread takes is.
MISTAKE 2
❌ The bridge were built in 1990.
✅ The bridge was built in 1990.
💡 The bridge is singular, and in 1990 is finished past.
MISTAKE 3
❌ The wall was paint yesterday.
✅ The wall was painted yesterday.
💡 The receiver the car becomes the grammatical subject.
MISTAKE 4
❌ The cake made by Dad (passive intended).
✅ The cake was made by Dad.
💡 By identifies the specific author.
