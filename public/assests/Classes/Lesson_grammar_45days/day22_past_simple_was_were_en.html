🗺️ GRAMMAR MAP: Past Simple: Was / Were (Be in Past)
Was with I/he/she/it
is → was
She was at the library yesterday.
💡 She is singular and yesterday places the state in the past.
am → was
I was tired after the match.
💡 I takes was, not were, in past statements.
Negatives: was not / were not
was → was not
The classroom was not noisy this morning.
💡 Not follows was; do not use did with was.
were → were not
They were not late for practice.
💡 Were not negates a plural past state.
Short answers
Was...? → Yes, ... was
Yes, she was.
💡 A Was question is answered with was, not did.
Were...? → No, ... were not
No, we were not.
💡 The short answer keeps were to agree with we.

📖 1. Detailed Rules & Spelling Patterns

Was with I/he/she/it

Use was for a singular past subject.

is → was
She was at the library yesterday.
💡 She is singular and yesterday places the state in the past.
am → was
I was tired after the match.
💡 I takes was, not were, in past statements.

Were with you/we/they

Use were for you and plural subjects.

are → were
We were at the zoo last Sunday.
💡 We is plural and last Sunday is finished.
are → were
You were very helpful yesterday.
💡 You takes were, whether it refers to one or several people.

Negatives: was not / were not

Add not directly after the past be verb.

was → was not
The classroom was not noisy this morning.
💡 Not follows was; do not use did with was.
were → were not
They were not late for practice.
💡 Were not negates a plural past state.

Questions: Was/Were + subject

Move was or were before the subject.

she was → Was she...?
Was she at home last night?
💡 Past be itself forms the question; did is unnecessary.
they were → Were they...?
Were they ready for the show?
💡 They pairs with were at the start of the question.

Short answers

Repeat was or were in yes/no answers.

Was...? → Yes, ... was
Yes, she was.
💡 A Was question is answered with was, not did.
Were...? → No, ... were not
No, we were not.
💡 The short answer keeps were to agree with we.

⚠️ 3. Common Mistakes: Wrong vs Right

MISTAKE 1
❌ She were tired.
✅ She was tired.
💡 She is singular and yesterday places the state in the past.
MISTAKE 2
❌ They was ready.
✅ They were ready.
💡 We is plural and last Sunday is finished.
MISTAKE 3
❌ I did not was happy.
✅ I was not happy.
💡 Not follows was; do not use did with was.
MISTAKE 4
❌ Did you were late?
✅ Were you late?
💡 Past be itself forms the question; did is unnecessary.

❓ Quick FAQ

Which form should I choose?

Use was for a singular past subject.

What is an important exception?

Repeat was or were in yes/no answers.

How can I practise this topic?

Compare the transformation line with the full sentence; explain the reason for each change before making a new sentence.
