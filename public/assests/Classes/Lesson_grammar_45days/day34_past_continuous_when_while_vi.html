🗺️ SƠ ĐỒ TƯ DUY: Past Continuous: Form & When / While
Was/were + V-ing
she + read → was reading
She was reading at eight last night.
💡 She dùng was; at eight xác định thời điểm quá khứ.
they + play → were playing
They were playing at noon.
💡 They đi với were; playing cho thấy hành động kéo dài.
Hành động bị chen ngang với when
was doing + when + rang
I was doing homework when the phone rang.
💡 Làm bài đang diễn ra; chuông reo đột ngột.
were eating + when + arrived
We were eating when Dad arrived.
💡 Bữa ăn đang diễn ra; bố về là sự kiện chen vào.
Câu hỏi với was/were
you were sleeping → Were you sleeping?
Were you sleeping at nine?
💡 Be quá khứ lên trước you; sleeping không đổi.
she was reading → Was she reading?
Was she reading then?
💡 She phải đi với was trong câu hỏi quá khứ tiếp diễn.

📖 1. Quy tắc chi tiết & Biến đổi dạng từ

Was/were + V-ing

Quá khứ tiếp diễn diễn tả việc đang diễn ra tại một thời điểm quá khứ.

she + read → was reading
She was reading at eight last night.
💡 She dùng was; at eight xác định thời điểm quá khứ.
they + play → were playing
They were playing at noon.
💡 They đi với were; playing cho thấy hành động kéo dài.

Phủ định quá khứ tiếp diễn

Thêm not sau was/were, giữ V-ing.

was sleeping → wasn’t sleeping
I wasn’t sleeping at ten.
💡 Not phủ định was; sleeping giữ -ing.
were watching → weren’t watching
We weren’t watching TV then.
💡 Weren’t phủ định hành động tiếp diễn với số nhiều.

Hành động bị chen ngang với when

Dùng quá khứ tiếp diễn cho nền và quá khứ đơn cho việc chen vào.

was doing + when + rang
I was doing homework when the phone rang.
💡 Làm bài đang diễn ra; chuông reo đột ngột.
were eating + when + arrived
We were eating when Dad arrived.
💡 Bữa ăn đang diễn ra; bố về là sự kiện chen vào.

While với hai hành động song song

While thường dẫn một hoạt động đang diễn ra làm bối cảnh.

while + was cooking
While Mum was cooking, I was setting the table.
💡 Hai hoạt động đang xảy ra cùng thời điểm.
while + were studying
The birds sang while we were studying.
💡 Học bài là hành động nền kéo dài.

Câu hỏi với was/were

Đưa was/were lên trước chủ ngữ.

you were sleeping → Were you sleeping?
Were you sleeping at nine?
💡 Be quá khứ lên trước you; sleeping không đổi.
she was reading → Was she reading?
Was she reading then?
💡 She phải đi với was trong câu hỏi quá khứ tiếp diễn.

🎯 2. Tình huống thực tế

Ở trường

She was reading at eight last night.
We weren’t watching TV then.

Ở nhà và với bạn bè

I was doing homework when the phone rang.
The birds sang while we were studying.

Kế hoạch và cuộc sống hằng ngày

Were you sleeping at nine?
They were playing at noon.

⚠️ 3. Lỗi thường gặp: Sai vs Đúng

LỖI 1
❌ She were reading.
✅ She was reading.
💡 She dùng was; at eight xác định thời điểm quá khứ.
LỖI 2
❌ They didn’t were sleeping.
✅ They weren’t sleeping.
💡 Not phủ định was; sleeping giữ -ing.
LỖI 3
❌ I was study when the bell rang.
✅ I was studying when the bell rang.
💡 Làm bài đang diễn ra; chuông reo đột ngột.
LỖI 4
❌ While I cooked, she was reading. (both continuous intended)
✅ While I was cooking, she was reading.
💡 Hai hoạt động đang xảy ra cùng thời điểm.
