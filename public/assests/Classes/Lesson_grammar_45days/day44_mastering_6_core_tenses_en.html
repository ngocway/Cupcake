📖 1. Detailed Rules & Spelling Patterns

Present simple: routines

Use the base or third-person -s for repeated habits.

she + read → reads
She reads books every evening.
💡 Every evening indicates routine; she requires -s.
we + play → play
We play chess on Sundays.
💡 We uses the base form for present routines.

Present continuous: now

Choose am/is/are + -ing for an action happening now.

now + cook → is cooking
Mum is cooking now.
💡 Now signals an ongoing event, so is cooking fits.
at the moment + run → are running
They are running at the moment.
💡 They takes are; run doubles n before -ing.

Past simple: completed events

Use V2 with a finished past-time marker.

go → went
He went home yesterday.
💡 Yesterday marks finished time; go changes to went.
watch → watched
We watched a film last night.
💡 Watch has a regular past form watched.

Past continuous: past in progress

Use was/were + -ing for an ongoing past background.

she + study → was studying
She was studying at nine last night.
💡 At nine last night locates an action in progress.
they + dance → were dancing
They were dancing when we arrived.
💡 The dancing was already in progress at arrival.

Future with will and going to

Select will for on-the-spot decisions; going to for existing intentions.

instant offer → will help
I will help you with the bags.
💡 The speaker volunteers at that moment.
prior plan → going to travel
We are going to travel next weekend.
💡 The journey is planned ahead.

Present perfect: past connected to now

Use have/has + V3 for experience, current results, and continuing states.

ever + see → have ever seen
Have you ever seen snow?
💡 Ever asks about any lifetime experience up to now.
since 2021 → has lived
She has lived here since 2021.
💡 Since 2021 marks when a continuing situation began.

❌ He read every night. (habit, singular he)
✅ He reads every night.
💡 Every evening indicates routine; she requires -s.
MISTAKE 2
❌ They are runing now.
✅ They are running now.
💡 Now signals an ongoing event, so is cooking fits.
MISTAKE 3
❌ We have visited the zoo yesterday.
✅ We visited the zoo yesterday.
💡 Yesterday marks finished time; go changes to went.
MISTAKE 4
❌ She were studying at nine.
✅ She was studying at nine.
