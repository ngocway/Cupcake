📖 1. Detailed Rules & Spelling Patterns

And adds information

And joins similar or additional ideas.

two details → and
I have a pencil and an eraser.
💡 And adds a second item of the same kind.
two activities → and
We sang and danced at the party.
💡 And links two activities that both happened.

But shows contrast

But connects ideas that contrast.

contrast → but
The bag is small, but it is heavy.
💡 Small and heavy create an unexpected contrast.
contrast → but
I wanted to play, but it started raining.
💡 The rain prevents the intended activity.

Or offers choices

Or presents alternatives.

choice → or
Would you like tea or juice?
💡 Or connects mutually offered drink choices.
choice → or
We can walk or take the bus.
💡 Or offers two possible ways to travel.

So introduces a result

So joins a cause followed by a result.

cause → so → result
It was cold, so we stayed inside.
💡 Cold weather causes the decision to stay indoors.
cause → so → result
I was hungry, so I made a sandwich.
💡 Hunger is the cause; making food is the result.

Because gives a reason

Because introduces the reason, not the result.

result → because → reason
We stayed home because it was raining.
💡 Because introduces the rain as the reason.
result → because → reason
She smiled because she passed the test.
💡 Passing the test explains her smile.

⚠️ 3. Common Mistakes: Wrong vs Right

MISTAKE 1
❌ I have a pen but a pencil.
✅ I have a pen and a pencil.
💡 And adds a second item of the same kind.
MISTAKE 2
❌ It is small and heavy (contrast intended).
✅ It is small but heavy.
💡 Small and heavy create an unexpected contrast.
MISTAKE 3
❌ Would you like milk and juice? (choose one)
✅ Would you like milk or juice?
💡 Or connects mutually offered drink choices.
MISTAKE 4
❌ It rained because we stayed in. (rain causes staying)
✅ It rained, so we stayed in.
💡 Cold weather causes the decision to stay indoors.
