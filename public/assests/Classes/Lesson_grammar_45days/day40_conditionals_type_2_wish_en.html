📖 1. Detailed Rules & Spelling Patterns

Second conditional: if + past, would + V1

Imagine an unlikely or unreal present/future situation.

have → had; will → would
If I had wings, I would fly.
💡 Had marks an unreal condition, not actual past time.
know → knew; will → would
If she knew the answer, she would tell us.
💡 Knew describes an imagined present condition.

If I were...

Were is preferred for formal unreal conditions with any subject.

I am → If I were
If I were you, I would ask the teacher.
💡 Were signals an unreal identity or situation.
she is → If she were
If she were taller, she would reach the shelf.
💡 Were presents imagined height, not past height.

Would + base verb

The result clause uses would with an unchanged base verb.

would + buy → would buy
I would buy a bike if I had enough money.
💡 Would expresses the hypothetical result; buy takes no -s.
would + help → would help
He would help if he had time.
💡 Even with he, the verb after would stays help.

Wish + past form for present wishes

Wish + past form expresses a present situation you want to change.

cannot → wish I could
I wish I could swim.
💡 Could describes a wished-for present ability.
do not have → wish I had
She wishes she had a pet.
💡 Had describes an unreal present possession.

Wish + were; do not use would in if clause

Use were for unreal being; keep would normally in the result clause.

wish + be → were
I wish I were taller.
💡 Were expresses an unreal present characteristic.
if I would have → if I had
If I had more time, I would travel.
💡 The if-clause takes had, not would have, in this basic pattern.

⚠️ 3. Common Mistakes: Wrong vs Right

MISTAKE 1
❌ If I would have wings, I would fly.
✅ If I had wings, I would fly.
💡 Had marks an unreal condition, not actual past time.
MISTAKE 2
❌ If I was you, I would ask. (formal hypothetical)
✅ If I were you, I would ask.
💡 Were signals an unreal identity or situation.
MISTAKE 3
❌ He would helps if he could.
✅ He would help if he could.
💡 Would expresses the hypothetical result; buy takes no -s.
MISTAKE 4
❌ I wish I can fly.
✅ I wish I could fly.
💡 Could describes a wished-for present ability.
