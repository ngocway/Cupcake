📖 1. Detailed Rules & Spelling Patterns

Can + base verb for present ability

Can is followed by a bare infinitive and does not add -s.

swim → can swim
My sister can swim very well.
💡 Can carries ability; swim stays unchanged after it.
draw → can draw
He can draw animals.
💡 Even after he, draw cannot become draws after can.

Cannot/can’t for inability

Place not with can; the next verb stays base form.

can fly → cannot fly
Penguins cannot fly.
💡 Cannot negates the ability; fly remains base form.
can play → can’t play
I can’t play the violin yet.
💡 Yet indicates the skill is not acquired at present.

Could for past general ability

Could + V1 describes an ability that existed previously.

can read → could read
At six, I could read short stories.
💡 At six locates a general past ability.
can climb → could climb
Grandpa could climb tall trees as a boy.
💡 Could expresses ability over a past period.

Could / couldn’t for polite requests

Could you...? is often softer than Can you...?

Can you help? → Could you help?
Could you help me, please?
💡 Could softens a request; help stays bare infinitive.
Can you open? → Could you open?
Could you open the window?
💡 Could asks politely, not about past ability here.

Modal questions and short answers

Invert can/could before the subject; answer with the same modal.

you can sing → Can you sing?
Can you sing this song?
💡 Can comes before you without do.
Can she...? → Yes, she can
Yes, she can.
💡 Repeat can rather than do in the short answer.

⚠️ 3. Common Mistakes: Wrong vs Right

MISTAKE 1
❌ He can swims.
✅ He can swim.
💡 Can carries ability; swim stays unchanged after it.
MISTAKE 2
❌ I can’t to play.
✅ I can’t play.
💡 Cannot negates the ability; fly remains base form.
MISTAKE 3
❌ I could to read at six.
✅ I could read at six.
💡 At six locates a general past ability.
MISTAKE 4
❌ Could you helping me?
✅ Could you help me?
💡 Could softens a request; help stays bare infinitive.
