📖 1. Detailed Rules & Spelling Patterns

Finished past time: past simple

Use past simple with yesterday, last week, in 2020.

yesterday + visit → visited
I visited the zoo yesterday.
💡 Yesterday identifies a finished time.
last year + buy → bought
We bought this bike last year.
💡 Last year is a definite completed past period.

Present relevance: present perfect

Use have/has + V3 for results relevant now without a finished-time marker.

lose → have lost
I have lost my keys.
💡 The keys are missing now, so the past action has a current result.
break → has broken
She has broken her glasses.
💡 The damaged glasses matter at present.

Already in affirmative sentences

Already emphasizes an action completed sooner than expected.

finish → have already finished
We have already finished the project.
💡 Already sits after have and before V3 finished.
eat → has already eaten
He has already eaten lunch.
💡 Has is used for he, and already comes before eaten.

Yet in questions and negatives

Yet often appears at the end of questions and negatives.

not finish → haven’t finished yet
I haven’t finished my drawing yet.
💡 Yet says completion is expected but has not happened.
have you done → Have you done ... yet?
Have you done it yet?
💡 Yet asks whether the expected event has happened up to now.

Ever and never for experience

Ever asks about experiences; never means at no time up to now.

ever + try → have ever tried
Have you ever tried sushi?
💡 Ever asks whether an experience has happened at any time in life.
never + ride → have never ridden
I have never ridden a horse.
💡 Never is already negative; do not add not.

⚠️ 3. Common Mistakes: Wrong vs Right

MISTAKE 1
❌ I have seen her yesterday.
✅ I saw her yesterday.
💡 Yesterday identifies a finished time.
MISTAKE 2
❌ I lost my keys (missing now emphasized).
✅ I have lost my keys.
💡 The keys are missing now, so the past action has a current result.
MISTAKE 3
❌ We have finished already the work.
✅ We have already finished the work.
💡 Already sits after have and before V3 finished.
MISTAKE 4
❌ I haven’t finished yet my work.
✅ I haven’t finished my work yet.
💡 Yet says completion is expected but has not happened.
