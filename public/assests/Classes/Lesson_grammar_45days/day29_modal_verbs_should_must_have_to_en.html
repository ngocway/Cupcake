🗺️ GRAMMAR MAP: Modal Verbs: Should, Must, Have To
Should: advice
rest → should rest
You should rest when you feel tired.
💡 Should recommends rest, without making it compulsory.
study → should study
We should study before the test.
💡 Should gives advice; study stays in base form.
Have to / has to: external necessity
she + have to → has to
She has to finish her homework.
💡 She is third-person singular, so have changes to has.
we + have to → have to
We have to arrive by eight.
💡 We takes have to, not has to.
Questions and past necessity
does + have to → Does ... have to?
Does she have to wear a uniform?
💡 After does, have stays base instead of has.
past have to → had to
We had to wait yesterday.
💡 Had to expresses past necessity for every subject.

📖 1. Detailed Rules & Spelling Patterns

Should: advice

Should + V1 recommends a sensible action.

rest → should rest
You should rest when you feel tired.
💡 Should recommends rest, without making it compulsory.
study → should study
We should study before the test.
💡 Should gives advice; study stays in base form.

Must: strong obligation

Must + V1 expresses a strong rule or necessity.

wear → must wear
You must wear a helmet on your bike.
💡 Must stresses a safety requirement; wear stays unchanged.
stop → must stop
Visitors must stop at the red line.
💡 Must indicates a rule rather than optional advice.

Have to / has to: external necessity

Choose has to with he/she/it; have to elsewhere.

she + have to → has to
She has to finish her homework.
💡 She is third-person singular, so have changes to has.
we + have to → have to
We have to arrive by eight.
💡 We takes have to, not has to.

Mustn’t vs don’t have to

Mustn’t prohibits; don’t have to means not necessary.

prohibition → mustn’t
You mustn’t touch that hot pan.
💡 Mustn’t says the action is forbidden.
optional → don’t have to
You don’t have to bring lunch today.
💡 Don’t have to removes an obligation; bringing lunch is still allowed.

Questions and past necessity

Use Do/Does ... have to? and had to for past obligations.

does + have to → Does ... have to?
Does she have to wear a uniform?
💡 After does, have stays base instead of has.
past have to → had to
We had to wait yesterday.
💡 Had to expresses past necessity for every subject.

⚠️ 3. Common Mistakes: Wrong vs Right

MISTAKE 1
❌ You should to rest.
✅ You should rest.
💡 Should recommends rest, without making it compulsory.
MISTAKE 2
❌ She must wears a helmet.
✅ She must wear a helmet.
💡 Must stresses a safety requirement; wear stays unchanged.
MISTAKE 3
❌ He have to leave.
✅ He has to leave.
💡 She is third-person singular, so have changes to has.
MISTAKE 4
❌ You mustn’t bring lunch means it is optional.
✅ You don’t have to bring lunch means it is optional.
💡 Mustn’t says the action is forbidden.
