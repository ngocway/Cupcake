Irregular past forms
go → went

💡 Go has the irregular past form went, not goed.
eat → ate
I ate an apple at break time.
💡 Eat changes to ate without -ed.
Negative: did not + base verb
went → did not go
He did not go to the park.
💡 Did already carries past tense, so go returns to base form.
ate → did not eat

💡 Did not requires eat, not ate.

📖 1. Detailed Rules & Spelling Patterns

Irregular past forms

Learn V2 forms rather than adding -ed.

go → went
We went to the museum yesterday.
💡 Go has the irregular past form went, not goed.
eat → ate
I ate an apple at break time.
💡 Eat changes to ate without -ed.
see → saw
Mia saw a rainbow.
💡 See uses irregular V2 saw.
buy → bought
Dad bought some bread.
💡 Buy changes to bought, not buyed.

Past affirmative with irregular V2

All subjects use the same irregular past form.

take → took
She took a photo yesterday.
💡 Past took does not take extra -s for she.
write → wrote
They wrote thank-you cards.
💡 Write changes to wrote for all subjects in the past.

Negative: did not + base verb

After did not or didn’t, use V1, not V2.

went → did not go
He did not go to the park.
💡 Did already carries past tense, so go returns to base form.
ate → did not eat
We did not eat the cake.
💡 Did not requires eat, not ate.

Questions: Did + subject + V1

Use did before the subject and the base verb afterward.

saw → Did ... see?
Did you see the new teacher?
💡 Did signals past time; see is base form.
bought → Did ... buy?
Did she buy a ticket?
💡 The main verb stays buy after did.

Short answers with did

Use did/didn’t instead of repeating an irregular past verb.

Did you...? → Yes, I did
Yes, I did.
💡 The auxiliary did answers a Did question.
Did they...? → No, they didn’t
No, they didn’t.
💡 A negative short answer uses didn’t with they.

⚠️ 3. Common Mistakes: Wrong vs Right

MISTAKE 1
❌ We goed to school.
✅ We went to school.
💡 Go has the irregular past form went, not goed.
MISTAKE 2
❌ She taked a photo.
✅ She took a photo.
💡 Past took does not take extra -s for she.
MISTAKE 3
❌ He didn’t went home.
✅ He didn’t go home.
💡 Did already carries past tense, so go returns to base form.
MISTAKE 4
❌ Did you saw him?
✅ Did you see him?
💡 Did signals past time; see is base form.

❓ Quick FAQ

Which form should I choose?

Learn V2 forms rather than adding -ed.

What is an important exception?

Use did/didn’t instead of repeating an irregular past verb.

How can I practise this topic?

Compare the transformation line with the full sentence; explain the reason for each change before making a new sentence.
