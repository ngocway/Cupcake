📖 1. Detailed Rules & Spelling Patterns

Past simple vs continuous

Identify a completed event versus an ongoing past background.

was reading + rang
I was reading when the bell rang.
💡 Reading was ongoing when the shorter bell event happened.
went → went
We went to the zoo last Saturday.
💡 Last Saturday is a finished past time.

Future plans vs instant decisions

Use going to for prior plans, will for decisions now.

prior plan → going to study
I am going to study tonight.
💡 The speaker has already chosen this plan.
instant offer → will carry
I will carry your bag.
💡 The offer is made at the moment of speaking.

Modal obligations and prohibition

Mustn’t prohibits; don’t have to removes necessity.

rule → must wear
You must wear a seat belt.
💡 Must communicates an important safety obligation.
optional → don’t have to
You don’t have to come early.
💡 Arriving early is optional, not prohibited.

Comparatives and superlatives

Compare two things with -er/more; a whole group with -est/most.

good → better
This book is better than that one.
💡 Good forms an irregular comparative better.
good → the best
This is the best book in the series.
💡 Best compares the item with a larger group.

Conjunctions of cause and result

Because gives a reason; so gives a result.

reason → because
I stayed home because I was ill.
💡 Illness is the reason for staying home.
result → so
I was ill, so I stayed home.
💡 Staying home is the consequence.

Gerunds and infinitives

Check which complement a verb requires.

enjoy → V-ing
She enjoys drawing animals.
💡 Enjoy takes a gerund.
want → to V
He wants to draw a map.
💡 Want takes a to-infinitive.

✅ I am going to study later.

MISTAKE 3
❌ You mustn’t come early (not required intended).
✅ You don’t have to come early.

MISTAKE 4
❌ She is more taller than me.
✅ She is taller than me.
