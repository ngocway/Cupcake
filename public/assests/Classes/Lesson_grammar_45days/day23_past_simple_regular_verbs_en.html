📖 1. Detailed Rules & Spelling Patterns

Add -ed to regular verbs

Most regular verbs add -ed in the past.

walk → walked
We walked to school yesterday.
💡 Walk has no special ending, so attach -ed.
clean → cleaned
Dad cleaned the kitchen last night.
💡 Clean forms the past with -ed without changing its spelling.

Verbs ending in -e: add -d

Do not add a second e.

dance → danced
The children danced at the party.
💡 Dance already ends in e, so add only d.
live → lived
She lived in Hue last year.
💡 Live takes -d, producing lived rather than liveed.

Consonant + y → -ied

Replace final y with i before -ed.

study → studied
He studied maths yesterday.
💡 The letter before y in study is consonant d, so y changes to i.
carry → carried
Mum carried the bags upstairs.
💡 Carry has consonant r before y, so use -ied.

Vowel + y → -yed

Keep y when a vowel comes immediately before it.

play → played
They played chess yesterday.
💡 A comes before y in play, so y stays.
enjoy → enjoyed
We enjoyed the picnic.
💡 O before y means add -ed without changing y.

Double consonants in stressed CVC

Double the final consonant when a short stressed vowel precedes it in an eligible CVC form.

stop → stopped
The bus stopped at the school gate.
💡 Stop ends in short o + p, so p doubles.
plan → planned
We planned a class trip.
💡 Plan ends in stressed short a + n, so n doubles.

Three -ed pronunciations

Use /ɪd/ after /t/ or /d/, /t/ after voiceless sounds, /d/ otherwise.

wanted → /ɪd/
I wanted a new notebook.
💡 Want ends in /t/, so -ed forms an extra /ɪd/ syllable.
washed → /t/
She washed her hands.
💡 Wash ends in voiceless /ʃ/, so -ed sounds /t/.
played → /d/
They played football.
💡 Play ends in voiced vowel sound, so -ed sounds /d/.

⚠️ 3. Common Mistakes: Wrong vs Right

MISTAKE 1
❌ We walkd home.
✅ We walked home.
💡 Walk has no special ending, so attach -ed.
MISTAKE 2
❌ She danceed.
✅ She danced.
💡 Dance already ends in e, so add only d.
MISTAKE 3
❌ He studyed hard.
✅ He studied hard.
💡 The letter before y in study is consonant d, so y changes to i.
MISTAKE 4
❌ They plaied outside.
✅ They played outside.
💡 A comes before y in play, so y stays.
