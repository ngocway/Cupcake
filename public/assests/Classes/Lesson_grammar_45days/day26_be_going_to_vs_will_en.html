📖 1. Detailed Rules & Spelling Patterns

Going to for prior plans

Use am/is/are going to + V1 for a plan decided before speaking.

plan → am going to visit
I am going to visit my cousin this weekend.
💡 The visit is an existing plan; I pairs with am.
plan → are going to make
We are going to make a model tomorrow.
💡 We takes are and the intended action uses base make.

Going to: evidence-based predictions

Use going to when present evidence points to a future result.

dark clouds → going to rain
Look at those clouds! It is going to rain.
💡 Dark clouds provide visible evidence of rain.
wobbling glass → going to fall
Watch out! The glass is going to fall.
💡 The unstable glass is evidence of an imminent fall.

Will for decisions made now

Use will when making an instant offer or decision.

need help → I will carry
You look tired. I will carry your books.
💡 The help is offered on the spot.
doorbell → I will open
Someone is at the door. I will open it.
💡 The speaker decides to open the door immediately.

Will for opinion-based predictions

Use will after expressions like I think or probably when guessing.

I think → will enjoy
I think you will enjoy this book.
💡 I think shows an opinion, not present proof.
probably → will pass
He will probably pass the test.
💡 Probably expresses likelihood without firm evidence given here.

Be agreement and negatives

Going to needs am/is/are; will stays the same for all subjects.

she + not → isn’t going to
She isn’t going to buy that toy.
💡 She needs is and not comes after is.
they + not → won’t
They won’t stay late.
💡 Won’t works with they without are.

⚠️ 3. Common Mistakes: Wrong vs Right

MISTAKE 1
❌ I going to visit Grandma.
✅ I am going to visit Grandma.
💡 The visit is an existing plan; I pairs with am.
MISTAKE 2
❌ It going to rain.
✅ It is going to rain.
💡 Dark clouds provide visible evidence of rain.
MISTAKE 3
❌ I will to answer it.
✅ I will answer it.
💡 The help is offered on the spot.
MISTAKE 4
❌ I think she going to win.
✅ I think she will win.
💡 I think shows an opinion, not present proof.

❓ Quick FAQ

Which form should I choose?

Use am/is/are going to + V1 for a plan decided before speaking.

What is an important exception?

Going to needs am/is/are; will stays the same for all subjects.
