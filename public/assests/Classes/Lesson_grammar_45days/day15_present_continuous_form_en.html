📖 1. Detailed Rules & Spelling Patterns

1. Am / is / are + verb-ing

Choose am for I, is for he/she/it, and are for you/we/they; put an -ing action after be.

I + read → am reading
I am reading a story now.
💡 I pairs with am; reading marks an action in progress.
They + play → are playing
They are playing chess at the moment.
💡 They takes are; playing shows the ongoing activity.
She + draw → is drawing
She is drawing a cat now.
💡 She takes is rather than are.

2. Add -ing to most verbs

For most verbs, attach -ing to the base form without another spelling change.

read → reading
Ben is reading comics.
💡 Read has no final silent -e or consonant-doubling pattern here, so add -ing.
cook → cooking
Dad is cooking dinner.
💡 Cook keeps its letters and simply adds -ing.

3. Drop final silent -e

With verbs ending in a silent -e, remove -e before adding -ing.

write → writing
Mia is writing a letter.
💡 Write ends with silent -e, which drops before -ing.
make → making
We are making paper flowers.
💡 Make loses its silent final -e before -ing.

4. Keep -ee and -ye endings

Do not drop the final -e from verbs ending in -ee or -ye.

see → seeing
We are seeing a play tonight.
💡 See ends in -ee; keep both e letters before -ing. Here seeing means attending a show.
dye → dyeing
They are dyeing fabric blue.
💡 Dye ends in -ye; retain the e to distinguish dyeing from dying.

5. Double a final consonant in short stressed CVC patterns

Double the last consonant after a stressed short vowel in eligible consonant-vowel-consonant patterns; do not double w, x, or y.

run → running
The dog is running across the yard.
💡 Run ends in stressed short vowel + n, so n doubles.
sit → sitting
The children are sitting on the rug.
💡 Sit has short i before final t, so t doubles.

6. Change -ie to -ying

For verbs ending in -ie, change ie to y and then add -ing.

lie → lying
The cat is lying on the sofa.
💡 Lie ends in -ie, which changes to y before -ing.
tie → tying
Sam is tying his shoes.
💡 Tie becomes tying rather than tieing.

7. Negatives and yes/no questions

Place not after am/is/are for negatives; move am/is/are before the subject to ask a question.

is painting → is not painting
She is not painting today.
💡 Not belongs after is, not between paint and -ing.
you are listening → Are you listening?
Are you listening to the teacher?
💡 Moving are before you forms a yes/no question while listening stays in -ing form.

8. Current actions vs temporary situations

Use the present continuous for something happening now or over a limited period around now.

now + study → am studying
I am studying for my quiz right now.
💡 Right now points to an activity in progress, so use am studying.
this week + stay → are staying
We are staying with Grandma this week.
💡 This week describes a temporary arrangement, not a permanent habit.

🎯 2. Real-World Usage Scenarios

At home

Mom is cooking, and Dad is washing the dishes.

⚠️ 3. Common Mistakes: Wrong vs Right

MISTAKE 1
❌ She are writing a story.
✅ She is writing a story.
💡 She requires is, not are.
MISTAKE 2
❌ He is makeing a kite.
✅ He is making a kite.
💡 Remove the silent final -e in make before adding -ing.
MISTAKE 3
❌ They are runing fast.
✅ They are running fast.
💡 Run doubles the final n before -ing.
MISTAKE 4
❌ Are you play outside?
✅ Are you playing outside?
💡 After are in a continuous question, use the -ing form.

❓ Quick FAQ

Does every -e verb lose e before -ing?

No. Keep -ee and -ye, as in seeing and dyeing.

Is “I am knowing” natural?

Usually no. Know is normally a stative verb: “I know the answer.”

Can “this week” be used with present continuous?

Yes, for a temporary situation: “We are staying here this week.”
