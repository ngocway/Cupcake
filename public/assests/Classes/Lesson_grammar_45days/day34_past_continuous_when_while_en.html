📖 1. Detailed Rules & Spelling Patterns

Was/were + V-ing

Past continuous describes an action in progress at a past moment.

she + read → was reading
She was reading at eight last night.
💡 She takes was, and at eight sets a past moment.
they + play → were playing
They were playing at noon.
💡 They pairs with were and playing shows duration.

Past continuous negative

Add not after was/were, keep V-ing.

was sleeping → wasn’t sleeping
I wasn’t sleeping at ten.
💡 Not negates was; sleeping remains in -ing.
were watching → weren’t watching
We weren’t watching TV then.
💡 Weren’t negates a plural past continuous action.

Interrupted action with when

Use past continuous for the background and past simple for the interruption.

was doing + when + rang
I was doing homework when the phone rang.
💡 Homework was in progress; the ring happened suddenly.
were eating + when + arrived
We were eating when Dad arrived.
💡 Eating is the background; arrival interrupts it.

While with simultaneous actions

While commonly introduces an ongoing background activity.

while + was cooking
While Mum was cooking, I was setting the table.
💡 Both activities were in progress at the same time.
while + were studying
The birds sang while we were studying.
💡 Studying was the longer background activity.

Questions with was/were

Move was/were before the subject.

you were sleeping → Were you sleeping?
Were you sleeping at nine?
💡 Past be moves before you; sleeping stays unchanged.
she was reading → Was she reading?
Was she reading then?
💡 She requires was in past continuous questions.

⚠️ 3. Common Mistakes: Wrong vs Right

MISTAKE 1
❌ She were reading.
✅ She was reading.
💡 She takes was, and at eight sets a past moment.
MISTAKE 2
❌ They didn’t were sleeping.
✅ They weren’t sleeping.
💡 Not negates was; sleeping remains in -ing.
MISTAKE 3
❌ I was study when the bell rang.
✅ I was studying when the bell rang.
💡 Homework was in progress; the ring happened suddenly.
MISTAKE 4
❌ While I cooked, she was reading. (both continuous intended)
✅ While I was cooking, she was reading.
💡 Both activities were in progress at the same time.

❓ Quick FAQ

Which form should I choose?

Past continuous describes an action in progress at a past moment.

What is an important exception?

Move was/were before the subject.

How can I practise this topic?
