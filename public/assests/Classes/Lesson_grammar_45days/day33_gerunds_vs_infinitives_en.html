🗺️ GRAMMAR MAP: Gerunds vs Infinitives (V-ing vs To V)
Enjoy/avoid/finish + V-ing
enjoy + read → enjoy reading
I enjoy reading comics.
💡 Enjoy selects reading, not to read, as its complement.
finish + clean → finish cleaning
We finished cleaning the room.
💡 Finish is followed by the -ing form of the next action.
After prepositions use V-ing
good at + draw → drawing
He is good at drawing.
💡 At is a preposition, so drawing follows it.
interested in + swim → swimming
She is interested in swimming.
💡 In is a preposition, and swim becomes swimming.
Stop/remember can change meaning
stop smoking → stop an activity
Dad stopped smoking last year.
💡 Stop + -ing means ending that activity.
stop to drink → pause for a purpose
We stopped to drink water.
💡 Stop + to V means pause another action in order to drink.

📖 1. Detailed Rules & Spelling Patterns

Enjoy/avoid/finish + V-ing

These verbs normally select a gerund complement.

enjoy + read → enjoy reading
I enjoy reading comics.
💡 Enjoy selects reading, not to read, as its complement.
finish + clean → finish cleaning
We finished cleaning the room.
💡 Finish is followed by the -ing form of the next action.

Want/hope/decide + to V

These verbs normally take to + base verb.

want + learn → want to learn
She wants to learn French.
💡 Want requires to before learn.
decide + join → decide to join
They decided to join the club.
💡 Decide usually takes a to-infinitive complement.

After prepositions use V-ing

A verb after a preposition takes gerund form.

good at + draw → drawing
He is good at drawing.
💡 At is a preposition, so drawing follows it.
interested in + swim → swimming
She is interested in swimming.
💡 In is a preposition, and swim becomes swimming.

Purpose: to + verb

To + infinitive can explain why someone acts.

go to shop → to buy
I went to the shop to buy milk.
💡 To buy explains the purpose of going to the shop.
study → to pass
He studies hard to pass the exam.
💡 To pass tells us the intended goal.

Stop/remember can change meaning

Some verbs take -ing or to V with a meaning difference.

stop smoking → stop an activity
Dad stopped smoking last year.
💡 Stop + -ing means ending that activity.
stop to drink → pause for a purpose
We stopped to drink water.
💡 Stop + to V means pause another action in order to drink.

⚠️ 3. Common Mistakes: Wrong vs Right

MISTAKE 1
❌ I enjoy to read.
✅ I enjoy reading.
💡 Enjoy selects reading, not to read, as its complement.
MISTAKE 2
❌ She wants learning English.
✅ She wants to learn English.
💡 Want requires to before learn.
MISTAKE 3
❌ He is good at draw.
✅ He is good at drawing.
💡 At is a preposition, so drawing follows it.
MISTAKE 4
❌ I went to the shop for buy milk.
✅ I went to the shop to buy milk.
💡 To buy explains the purpose of going to the shop.
