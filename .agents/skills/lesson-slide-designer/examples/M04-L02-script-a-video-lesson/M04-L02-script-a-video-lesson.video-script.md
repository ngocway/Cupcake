### S06 · cards · 22 s

**On screen:**
- Six principles of a video lesson
- One video, one idea: Under ten minutes, its own open and close
- Show, don't read: Never read the slide out loud
- Cut the extras: Music, fly-ins, decorative text
- Point at the key part: Circle, zoom, highlight as you speak
- Talk to one person: Say you, keep it short
- Learners must act: A question and a pause in each video

**Production notes:** Reveal the cards one by one with the narration.

**Narration:**

```
Here are the six.
One video teaches one idea.
Pictures add to your words, they do not repeat them.
Cut everything that does not serve the objective.
Point at exactly what you are talking about.
Talk to one person, as if they were sitting next to you.
And make the learner do something, not just listen.
```

### S13 · process · 19 s

**On screen:**
- Six steps to a script
- Step 1: Fix the objective
- Step 2: Cut into segments
- Step 3: Write the voice
- Step 4: Pair each segment
- Step 5: Add interaction
- Step 6: Read aloud, time it

**Production notes:** Reveal each step with its arrow as the narration reaches it.

**Narration:**

```
Six steps.
Fix the objective in one sentence.
Cut the lesson into eight to fifteen segments.
Write what you will say, in spoken words.
Pair each segment with one slide.
Add at least two pauses where the learner does something.
Then read it out loud and time it.
```
